Iron Man

About Iron Man

Iron Man is one of my favourite superheroes from Marvel. His real name is Tony Stark. He is really smart and loves making new things. What I find cool is that he actually builds his own suit instead of having superpowers.

Why I Like Him

I like Iron Man because he is smart, funny and a little crazy sometimes. He doesn't always make the right decision, but he learns from his mistakes. I also really like the way he uses technology to solve problems.

Things I Like About Iron Man

  • His amazing suits
  • His intelligence
  • His funny personality
  • The way he helps people

My Favourite Thing

My favourite thing about Iron Man is his character development. He changes a lot throughout the movies and becomes more responsible. His story is one of the reasons why I enjoy watching him so much.
